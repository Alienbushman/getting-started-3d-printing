---
term: "TPU"
definition: "Thermoplastic Polyurethane — a flexible, rubber-like filament used for cases, gaskets, grips, and anything that needs to bend or compress without breaking. Prints at 220–240°C. Requires a direct-drive extruder for reliable feeding — Bowden setups struggle with flexible filaments. Not a beginner material, but a useful addition once you're comfortable with PLA."
see_also: ["pla", "petg", "extruder"]
