---
term: "Bridge / Bridging"
definition: "A horizontal span printed over open air, connecting two supported walls. Unlike a straight overhang, a bridge has support on both ends. FDM printers can bridge surprisingly well — up to 80 mm with good cooling — by extruding fast and cooling the plastic quickly mid-air."
see_also: ["overhang", "support", "part-cooling-fan"]
