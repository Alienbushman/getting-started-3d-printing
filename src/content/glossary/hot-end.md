---
term: "Hot end"
definition: "The heated assembly at the front of the print head that melts filament. Contains a heater block (heats to print temperature), a thermistor (measures temperature), a heat break (transition between hot and cold zones), and a nozzle. The hot end is one of the most common parts to maintain or replace."
see_also: ["extruder", "nozzle-temperature", "fdm"]
