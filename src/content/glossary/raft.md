---
term: "Raft"
definition: "A thick multi-layer platform printed under the entire model, separated from it by a thin gap layer. The raft improves bed adhesion and compensates for an unlevel bed, but adds print time and leaves a rough surface on the bottom of the model."
see_also: ["brim", "bed-adhesion"]
gotcha: "Rafts are rarely needed with modern auto-leveling printers and PEI beds. Try a brim first — it's faster to print and easier to remove."
---
