---
term: "Extruder"
definition: "The motor-driven mechanism that grips and pushes filament toward the hot end at a controlled rate. A 'direct drive' extruder is mounted on the print head directly above the hot end. A 'Bowden' extruder is mounted on the frame and connected to the hot end by a PTFE tube — lighter but with more retraction lag."
see_also: ["hot-end", "retraction", "fdm"]
