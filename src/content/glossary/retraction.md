---
term: "Retraction"
definition: "A slicer setting that pulls the filament back a small distance (typically 0.5–2 mm for direct drive, 4–7 mm for Bowden) before a travel move. This reduces ooze from the nozzle and prevents strings from forming between features."
see_also: ["stringing", "z-hop"]
gotcha: "Too much retraction can cause under-extrusion or gaps on the next extrusion move. Tune retraction in small increments."
---
