---
term: "PETG"
definition: "Polyethylene Terephthalate Glycol — a tougher, slightly flexible filament that sits between PLA and ABS in difficulty. Prints at 230–245°C with a 70–85°C bed, no enclosure needed. More resistant to impact and heat than PLA (up to ~80°C). Moisture-sensitive — store sealed with desiccant."
see_also: ["pla", "abs", "stringing"]
gotcha: "PETG strings more than PLA. Lower nozzle temperature and increase retraction slightly to reduce stringing."
---
