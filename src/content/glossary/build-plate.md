---
term: "Build plate / Bed"
definition: "The flat surface the print adheres to during printing. Usually heated to improve adhesion and reduce warping. Modern printers use a removable spring-steel sheet with PEI coating — flex the sheet to pop off finished prints. Keep it clean with isopropyl alcohol between prints."
see_also: ["bed-adhesion", "pei-sheet", "bed-temperature"]
---
