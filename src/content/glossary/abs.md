---
term: "ABS"
definition: "Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene — a strong, impact-resistant, and heat-tolerant filament (up to ~100°C). Requires an enclosed printer and 90–110°C bed to prevent warping. Produces fumes during printing — always print in a ventilated space or with an enclosure that filters air. Not recommended as a first filament."
see_also: ["pla", "petg", "warping"]
gotcha: "ABS will warp almost every time without an enclosure. If you don't have an enclosure, use PETG instead for functional parts."
---
