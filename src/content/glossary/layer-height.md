---
term: "Layer height"
definition: "The thickness of each printed layer, measured in millimetres. A standard layer height for beginners is 0.2 mm — this balances surface quality with print speed. Thinner layers (0.1 mm) produce smoother surfaces but take much longer; thicker layers (0.3 mm) print faster but show more visible lines."
see_also: ["fdm", "print-speed"]
