---
term: "FDM (Fused Deposition Modeling)"
definition: "The most common desktop 3D printing process. A heated nozzle melts plastic filament and deposits it one layer at a time until a solid object is built up. This is the process used by Prusa, Bambu Lab, Creality, and most other consumer printers."
see_also: ["hot-end", "extruder", "layer-height"]
---
