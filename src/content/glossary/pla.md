---
term: "PLA"
definition: "Polylactic Acid — the most beginner-friendly FDM filament. Prints at 200–215°C with a 60°C bed, requires no enclosure, and sticks well to PEI. Biodegradable under industrial composting conditions. The main weakness: low heat resistance (softens around 60°C), making it unsuitable for hot environments."
