---
term: "Stringing"
definition: "Thin hairs of plastic that form between features of a print when the nozzle travels over open air and oozes. Caused by too-high nozzle temperature or insufficient retraction. Fix: lower nozzle temperature by 5°C, or increase retraction distance by 0.5 mm increments."
see_also: ["retraction", "nozzle-temperature", "z-hop"]
---
