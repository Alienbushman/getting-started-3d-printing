---
term: "Elephant's foot"
definition: "A defect where the bottom of the print flares outward wider than the rest of the model. Caused by the first layer being over-squished (Z offset too low) or the bed temperature being too high, keeping the plastic soft for too long. Fix: raise Z offset in 0.05 mm increments."
see_also: ["first-layer", "bed-temperature"]
---
