---
term: "Warping"
definition: "When the base or corners of a print lift off the build plate during printing. Caused by the outer edges of the print cooling and contracting faster than the interior, creating tension that pulls the base upward. Fix: clean the bed, raise bed temperature, add a brim, or enclose the printer (essential for ABS)."
see_also: ["bed-adhesion", "brim", "abs"]
---
