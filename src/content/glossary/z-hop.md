---
term: "Z-hop"
definition: "A slicer option that lifts the nozzle slightly (typically 0.2–0.4 mm) before travel moves, then lowers it back down at the destination. Prevents the nozzle from dragging across already-printed features and knocking them loose."
see_also: ["retraction"]
gotcha: "Enabling Z-hop can increase print time noticeably and may cause stringing on some materials. Disable it first and only enable if you see the nozzle dragging."
---
