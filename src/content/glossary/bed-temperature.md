---
term: "Bed temperature"
definition: "The target temperature of the build plate, measured in °C. Typical values: PLA 55–65°C, PETG 70–85°C, ABS 90–110°C. Bed heat keeps the first layer plastic long enough to bond properly and prevents the base of the print from contracting and warping."
see_also: ["bed-adhesion", "warping", "build-plate"]
---
