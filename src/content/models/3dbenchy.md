## Why print this

3DBenchy is the most widely printed calibration model in the world. Every printer owner has a reference photo of a well-printed Benchy — which means you can compare your result to thousands of others. It tests the full range of printer capabilities: curved hull, name tag bridge, chimney overhang, and fine window detail, all without supports.

## Before you slice

No special settings needed. Use your printer's default PLA profile at 0.2 mm layer height and 15% infill. No supports required — the overhangs are all within 45°.

Orientation: the model should be flat on the bed with the hull facing up. This is the default orientation in most slicer imports.

## What to expect

1. Load PLA and heat the hot end to 215°C, bed to 60°C.
2. Import the STL into your slicer. Use the default printer profile.
3. Slice at 0.2 mm layer height, 15% infill, no supports.
4. Watch the first layer. The skirt should extrude cleanly; the hull base should lie flat.
5. The print takes approximately 1 hour 40 minutes at default speed.
6. Let the bed cool fully before removing. The Benchy should pop off a PEI sheet without tools.
7. Compare to reference photos online. Look at: chimney overhang quality, bridge under the bow, layer consistency on the curved hull.

## If it goes wrong

- **Stringy cabin interior** — nozzle temperature too high or retraction too low. Lower temp by 5°C.
- **Chimney not printing cleanly** — cooling fan issue. Check the fan is spinning at full speed on small features.
- **Hull warping at the bow** — bed adhesion problem. Clean the PEI sheet with IPA and re-level.
- **Wobbly or uneven layer lines on the hull** — Z-banding. Check lead screw coupling and lubrication.
